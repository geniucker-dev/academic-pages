I'm pursuing my dual Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) and the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/). My research interests are machine learning and power system optimization.

I am the first author of **T-SKM-Net**, published at AAAI 2026. Our work integrates the Sampling Kaczmarz-Motzkin method into a trainable neural network framework for linear constraint satisfaction, with applications to DC optimal power flow.
